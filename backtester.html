Strategy logic — Range Breakdown:
1. For each day, identify the chosen instrument (NIFTY or SENSEX)
2. Lock ATM strike at REF_END time
3. Compute CE/PE Low and High over [REF_START, REF_END]
4. After REF_END: limit SELL at ref Low
5. SL = ref High; on SL, wait for premium to fall back to Low and re-enter
6. Exit at exit time
Note: Brokerage applied per leg-trade (entry + exit round trip). Slippage applied as points × lot size per leg-trade. Tune both to match your real costs.
